# Muscle-focused kettlebell program

Equipment

* 1 × 12 kg kettlebell
* 1 × 16 kg kettlebell
* 1 × 20 kg kettlebell

Difficulty: Intermediate
Focus: Build and retain muscle while losing fat.
Name: Muscle

Weekly Schedule

Day	Workout
Monday	Workout A
Wednesday	Workout B
Friday	Workout A

---

Warm-Up

Repeat for 5 minutes:

1. March or jog on the spot — 30 seconds
2. Slow bodyweight squats — 15 seconds
3. Arm circles — 15 seconds

---

Workout A

1. Goblet Squat
    * Sets: 3
    * Reps: 8–15
    * Rest: 90 seconds
    * Suggested bell: 16 kg
    * Notes: Controlled descent. Drive up hard.
2. Single Arm Row
    * Sets: 3
    * Reps: 8–15
    * Target: 8–15 per side
    * Rest: 90 seconds
    * Suggested bell: 16 kg
    * Notes: Stable torso. Pause at the top.
3. Push Up or Floor Press
    * Sets: 3
    * Reps: 8–15
    * Rest: 90 seconds
    * Notes: Full push-ups, or one-arm floor press per side
4. Single-Leg or B-Stance Romanian Deadlift
    * Sets: 3
    * Reps: 8–12
    * Target: 8–12 per side
    * Rest: 90 seconds
    * Suggested bell: 16 kg
    * Notes: Hips back. Do not turn it into a squat.
5. Suitcase Hold or Carry
    * Sets: 3
    * Work: 90 seconds
    * Rest: 90 seconds
    * Target: 30–60s each side
    * Suggested bell: 16–20 kg
    * Notes: Heaviest bell you can hold without leaning

Exercise Glossary

Goblet Squat

1. Hold the kettlebell at chest height with both hands.
2. Keep your elbows tucked.
3. Sit your hips back and down between your knees.
4. Keep your chest up.
5. Drive through your heels to stand tall.

Controlled descent. Full range you can maintain.

---

Single Arm Kettlebell Row

1. Hinge forward with a neutral spine.
2. Grab the kettlebell with one hand.
3. Support yourself with your free hand if needed.
4. Pull the kettlebell toward your hip.
5. Keep your elbow close to your body.
6. Lower it under control.

Pause briefly at the top. Keep the torso stable.

---

Push Up

1. Start in a high plank.
2. Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder width.
3. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
4. Lower your chest toward the floor.
5. Press through your palms to return to the start.

If these are easy: slower lowering, pause at the bottom, feet elevation, or a weight.

---

Floor Press

1. Lie on your back with the kettlebell in one hand.
2. Upper arm on the floor, elbow about 45 degrees from your body.
3. Press until the arm is straight.
4. Lower until the upper arm meets the floor.
5. Finish the set, then switch sides.

---

Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift

1. Hold the kettlebell in one or both hands.
2. Stand on one leg with a soft knee.
3. Hinge at the hips and send the free leg back.
4. Keep a long spine.
5. Feel the hamstring and glute on the standing leg.
6. Drive the hips forward to stand.

Do not turn it into a squat.

---

B-Stance Romanian Deadlift

1. Stagger your stance: front foot flat, back foot on the toes.
2. Most of your weight on the front leg.
3. Hold the kettlebell and hinge at the hips.
4. Keep a long spine.
5. Drive the hips forward to stand.
6. Finish the set, then switch the stance.

---

Suitcase Hold

1. Stand tall holding the kettlebell by your side in one hand.
2. Keep your shoulders level.
3. Brace your core.
4. Avoid leaning to either side.
5. Switch hands halfway through the set.

---

Suitcase Carry

1. Stand tall holding the kettlebell by your side.
2. Walk slowly with a braced core.
3. Keep shoulders and hips level.
4. Switch hands halfway through the set.

---

Reverse Goblet Lunge

1. Hold the kettlebell at chest height with both hands.
2. Keep your elbows tucked.
3. Step one leg backward.
4. Lower both knees toward roughly 90 degrees.
5. Drive through your front heel to return to standing.
6. Repeat with the opposite leg.

Rack position is fine if the goblet hold is uncomfortable.

---

Single Arm Kettlebell Clean & Press

1. Clean the kettlebell into the rack position at your shoulder.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the kettlebell overhead until your arm is fully extended.
4. Lower it under control to the rack position.
5. Repeat, then switch sides.

No excessive back lean. Do not rush to 20 kg.

---

Kettlebell Swing

1. Place the kettlebell slightly in front of you.
2. Hinge at your hips with a slight bend in your knees.
3. Grab the kettlebell with both hands.
4. Hike it backward between your legs.
5. Snap your hips forward powerfully.
6. Let the kettlebell float to about chest height.
7. Keep your arms relaxed.
8. Let the kettlebell fall naturally into the next hip hinge.

Stop the set if the arms start lifting the bell, your back position changes, or the hip snap loses power.

---

Suitcase March

1. Hold the kettlebell at your side in one hand.
2. Stand tall and brace your core.
3. March slowly in place.
4. Keep your shoulders and hips level.
5. Switch hands halfway through the set.
