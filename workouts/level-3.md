# **Exercise Glossary**

## **Goblet Squat**

1. Hold the kettlebell at chest height with both hands.
2. Keep your elbows tucked.
3. Sit your hips back and down between your knees.
4. Keep your chest up.
5. Drive through your heels to stand tall.

---

## **Goblet Squat → 2 Lunges**

One sequence consists of:

1. Hold the kettlebell in the goblet position.
2. Perform **1 goblet squat**.
3. Return to standing.
4. Step one leg backward and perform **1 reverse lunge**.
5. Return to standing.
6. Step the other leg backward and perform **1 reverse lunge**.
7. Return to standing and repeat.

**Sequence:** squat → right lunge → left lunge

---

## **Single Arm Kettlebell Clean**

1. Start with the kettlebell on the floor slightly in front of you.
2. Hinge at your hips and grab the handle with one hand.
3. Use a powerful hip drive to bring the kettlebell upward.
4. Guide it toward your shoulder.
5. Allow it to rotate smoothly around your wrist.
6. Finish in the rack position at shoulder height.

Avoid letting the kettlebell slam into your forearm.

---

## **Clean & Press (2 hands)**

1. Start with the kettlebell on the floor.
2. Hinge at your hips and grab the handle with both hands.
3. Use a strong hip drive to bring the kettlebell to chest height.
4. Brace your core.
5. Press the kettlebell overhead.
6. Lower it under control.

---

## **Kettlebell Swing**

1. Place the kettlebell slightly in front of you.
2. Hinge at your hips with a slight bend in your knees.
3. Grab the kettlebell with both hands.
4. Hike it backward between your legs.
5. Snap your hips forward powerfully.
6. Let the kettlebell float to about chest height.
7. Keep your arms relaxed.
8. Let the kettlebell fall naturally into the next hip hinge.

The movement should be driven primarily by your hips rather than your arms.

---

## **Kneeling Push Up**

1. Start on your knees.
2. Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder width.
3. Keep your body in a straight line from knees to shoulders.
4. Lower your chest toward the floor.
5. Press through your palms to return to the start.

---

## **Ballistic Kettlebell Row**

1. Hinge at your hips with a flat back.
2. Hold the kettlebell with one hand.
3. Brace your core.
4. Pull the kettlebell explosively toward your ribs.
5. Lower it under control.
6. Repeat.

---

## **Single Arm Kettlebell Row**

1. Hinge forward with a neutral spine.
2. Grab the kettlebell with one hand.
3. Support yourself with your free hand if needed.
4. Pull the kettlebell toward your hip.
5. Keep your elbow close to your body.
6. Lower it under control.

---

## **Dead Bug**

1. Lie on your back.
2. Extend your arms toward the ceiling.
3. Bend your knees to approximately 90 degrees.
4. Keep your lower back pressed toward the floor.
5. Slowly lower one arm and the opposite leg.
6. Return to the starting position.
7. Repeat on the other side.

---

## **Crunch**

1. Lie on your back with your knees bent.
2. Keep your feet on the floor.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Lift your head and upper shoulders slightly off the floor.
5. Lower yourself slowly.
6. Repeat.

---

## **Single Arm Kettlebell Clean & Press**

1. Clean the kettlebell into the rack position at your shoulder.
2. Brace your core.
3. Press the kettlebell overhead until your arm is fully extended.
4. Lower it under control to the rack position.
5. Repeat.

For the **alternating** version:

1. Perform the clean and press on one side.
2. Bring the kettlebell down under control.
3. Switch hands.
4. Perform the next repetition on the other side.
5. Continue alternating.
