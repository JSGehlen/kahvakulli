Exercise Glossary

Goblet Squat

1. Hold the kettlebell at chest height with both hands.
2. Keep your elbows tucked.
3. Sit your hips back and down between your knees.
4. Keep your chest up.
5. Drive through your heels to stand tall.

---

Clean & Press (2 hands)

1. Start with the kettlebell on the floor.
2. Hinge at your hips and grab the handle with both hands.
3. Use a strong hip drive to bring the kettlebell to chest height.
4. Brace your core.
5. Press the kettlebell overhead.
6. Lower it under control.

---

Kettlebell Row

1. Hinge at your hips with a flat back.
2. Hold the kettlebell handle with both hands.
3. Pull it toward your lower ribs.
4. Keep your shoulders down.
5. Keep your core braced.
6. Lower it under control.

---

Kettlebell Deadlift

1. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart.
2. Place the kettlebell between your feet.
3. Hinge at your hips while keeping your back flat.
4. Grab the handle with both hands.
5. Drive through your heels.
6. Squeeze your glutes as you stand tall.
7. Lower the kettlebell by pushing your hips back.

---

Kneeling Push Up

1. Start on your knees.
2. Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder width.
3. Keep your body in a straight line from knees to shoulders.
4. Lower your chest toward the floor.
5. Press through your palms to return to the start.

---

Thruster (2 hands)

1. Hold the kettlebell at chest height with both hands.
2. Squat down while keeping your chest tall.
3. Drive upward explosively.
4. Use the momentum from your legs to press the kettlebell overhead.
5. Lower it back to chest height.
6. Repeat.

---

Suitcase Hold

1. Stand tall holding the kettlebell by your side in one hand.
2. Keep your shoulders level.
3. Brace your core.
4. Avoid leaning to either side.
5. Maintain an upright posture.

For a 30-second interval:

* 15 seconds right
* 15 seconds left
